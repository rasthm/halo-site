Questions owners ask.

Do you only build AI?

No. We build websites, web and phone apps, automations, integrations, APIs and company brains. Many teams start with a website or one automation and add the rest once the first piece pays off.

How do you learn our business?

A free call first, then an hour with the person who does the work. We write down how it really happens, step by step, before we propose anything.

What do we actually gain, and when?

For a website, enquiries; for automations, hours back and money saved, measured against a baseline we write down in the first week.

Who owns what you build?

You do. The code, the data and the accounts it runs on are yours, and we can set it up in your own cloud account so you hold the keys.

Where does our data live?

In a private store set up for your company only, with access rules per role and an audit log. Nothing is used to train public models.
