# Motion inspiration (owner's picks, 18 Nov 2026)

Studied with Playwright (libraries, scroll screenshots, frame bursts). We take techniques, never their look, copy or assets.

## melon.studio (Next.js, WebGL canvases, Early Sans + Geist Mono)
- Hero: huge blackletter wordmark under a red-to-black gradient blob that drifts; small "I design and build…" line; live local time + "Open to new projects".
- Work list: project images are WebGL planes that bend and curve with scroll velocity (cylinder-like warp), straightening when the scroll stops.
- Sections switch colour themes as you scroll (black → bone → grey → black → red).
- Words in headings smear/distort in red ("Experience", "What people say", the about paragraph).
- Clients wall as a hairline grid; testimonial carousel; footer with giant email wordmark that waves.

## inacionakiti.com (Vite, Lenis smooth scroll, many canvases, General Sans)

- Name marquee in particle text.
- "What I do": pinned section, tabs 01–06 with progress bars; each service has a particle illustration (integration hub, gauge, wireframe) that morphs into the next.
- "From first call to long after launch": dark pinned section, waveform → hub → cloud particle morphs while process steps highlight.
- Selected work: full-bleed dithered image backgrounds, round "Visit site" cursor.
- Experience: giant year counter (2017 → 2024) scrubbed by scroll beside a timeline.
- Motto with an interactive "Break it" trick; a left-edge scroll ruler; floating site controls.
