Scope: the whole Halo Automations site, second redesign (replaces the neon world); home is the lead surface. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: owners of small and growing businesses, India first, often on a phone. Job: see that one team builds everything, believe it pays back, book a call or WhatsApp.
Proof: shipped projects, the savings estimate on their own numbers, stated timelines. No testimonials or client numbers exist.
Owner choices (18 Nov 2026): palette open, logo shape kept and recoloured, structure open; rejected neon, crafty/retro and white grounds; wants a deep rich colour, premium and confident; picked Marine Drive Deco but not green, chose aubergine. Avoid navy-and-gold (Ant Venture), generic AI-startup look, corporate coldness, busy/long pages.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Halo as a Marine Drive Art Deco building: one confident studio whose lobby directory lists every service on its own floor. Refuses the split hero over a card grid, and the dark-glow tech look.

OWN-WORLD: Aubergine walls (#2E1A3D, deeper #24132F, panels #3C2350), brass hairlines, stepped ziggurat frame tops and sunburst rays in #C9A24A, ivory #F0E6D0 type, one coral #E5533D for the single action. Big Shoulders Display (tall condensed deco caps) for headlines, Jost (1920s geometric) for reading; brass tabular numerals for every figure. Logo ring in brass.

STORY: The visitor sees one studio builds websites, apps, automations, integrations and a company brain; reads the lobby directory floor by floor with each service's timeline and the number they measure; checks the savings on their own figures; books a call or WhatsApps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns: a stepped brass frame holding the headline "Everything your business runs on." in big deco caps, one line of copy, coral Book a call and WhatsApp us. Right five columns: a deco lift floor-indicator dial (semicircle, brass numerals 1 to 6, a needle) above the lobby directory listing the six services as floors with their live-in times. Faint sunburst rays rise from the lower left.

FORM: grounded candidate 1 (Marine Drive Deco), taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned Eclipse, recoloured from emerald to aubergine at the owner's request; seed: 346fc7182f0f64ed. Signature interaction: the lift indicator: its needle swings to the floor of the service you hover, focus or scroll to, and that floor's brass rule lights. Reduced motion: the needle jumps without sweeping.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
